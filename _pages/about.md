# About Me

Hello, I'm Xiwei Zhao. My work explores the intersection of AI and robotics, where I focus on training intelligent agents through imitation learning. I'm driven by the challenge of creating autonomous systems that can learn from and robustly adapt to our world.

When I'm not in the lab, I find inspiration in reading, hiking, and the rich complexities of classical music. I believe this blend of analytical and creative pursuits is essential for innovation.

I am looking for my next challenge: a Ph.D. program or an industrial R&D role in Robotics or Applied AI for the Spring 2026 term.
